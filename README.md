# EJSY_StelLFHthon

We have created an odd-one-out game for children with cerebral palsy as part of the Singtel x LionsForge Inclusive Hackathon 2026.

## Background

To create a carnival game for children with cerebral palsy, we first sought to empathise with the challenges and difficulties they may face.

We found that many children with cerebral palsy have a very diverse range of motor abilities. They may have spastic, dyskinetic, and/or ataxic muscle movements, affecting one or more parts of parts of their body. They may also prefer one side of their body for movements, and/or have limited fine motor skills and range of motion. In addition, they also have a very diverse range of cognitive abilities.

We therefore defined that our game must cater to a wide variety of needs. The game must also enable and encourage the participation of the child, and cannot be time sensitive or require fine motor skills.

## The game

Our game is simple in concept, but it is highly customizable and modular to the needs of each child.

The goal of the game is simple: to find the odd one out among a number of options presented. These options can take form of images/shapes, audio played, or a tactile surface that the child can touch. The game can even present a combination of these stimuli to the child. This is to allow children with impaired vision, impaired hearing, or a combination of both to all be able to participate in our game.

There are two modes which the child can use to input their choice. Either select mode, where there is an input/switche corresponding to each of the options, or scanning mode, where the game will select/highlight options sequentially for the child the choose using a single input/switch. The child may use one of the buttons we have, or connect their own switch using the standard 3.5mm mono audio jack.
